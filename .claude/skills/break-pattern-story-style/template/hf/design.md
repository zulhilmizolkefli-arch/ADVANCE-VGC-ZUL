# VGC Break Pattern Ad: design

Concept angle: a messy creator's desk that turns into a clean pipeline. Cut-out character and flat paper cards on a light stage, flipping to navy/charcoal when the work piles up, mustard when the idea lands.

## Palette (graphics/text only; footage and product screenshots keep true colour)
- Paper (off-white): #F2EEE6 (light stage), Paper-2 #E6E0D2
- Ink: #14171F
- Navy: #1B2236, Charcoal: #171A21
- Mustard (accent, taken from the character's sweater): #E09827, light #F0B64A
- Rule: one accent colour. No neon.

## Type
- Archivo Black 400 for impact words (tight tracking -0.03em).
- JetBrains Mono 700 for UI chips / labels (uppercase).
- Min size in-feed: 44 px labels, 110 px+ punchlines.

## Layout
- 1080x1920, side safe area 8-10% for text (x 80-1000), key text between y 160 and 1650.
- Neo-paper cards: 4 px ink border, hard 10 px ink offset shadow, radius 24.

## Motion
- Pops: back.out 1.6-2 (0.35-0.45 s); slams: power4.out from scale 1.6-2.2; wipes: mustard block 0.35 s.
- Alternation: dense (S2/S3) -> empty (S4) -> quiet (S5) -> bright reveal (S6).
