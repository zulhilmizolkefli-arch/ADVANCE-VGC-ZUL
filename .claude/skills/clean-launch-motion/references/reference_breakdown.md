# Reference breakdown: "Best SaaS Product Launch Ad Video | LangEase" (Zelios)

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SgmuplXU2iY. Inspected on 27 Sep 2026 in a browser.
Nothing from the reference (footage, VO, soundtrack, branding) is embedded in the client's ad or saved in this project.

## How it was inspected
- A YouTube pre-roll ad played first ("Master Claude in 28 days", then a second ad). It was skipped or waited out and is excluded here. The reference itself is **33.06 s, 1280x720** (reported by the player).
- **Motion:** 66 frames were sampled about every 0.5 s during 0.5x playback and reviewed as contact sheets.
- **Colours and sizes:** measured from full-resolution 1280x720 canvas reads at 13 timestamps (mode background colour, ink-colour clusters, bounding boxes).
- **Limits:** motion was not reviewed frame by frame at 30 fps. Sub-0.5 s easing curves are estimated from adjacent samples. The soundtrack and VO were **not** analysed; the pane was muted, and only pacing was inferred from the cut rhythm.

## Palette (sampled, 8-bit sRGB from the decoded YouTube stream)
- Background (mode, every scene): **#FAF6FE**
- Blue gradient words/buttons: darkest **#2B7CF5**, most frequent **#4898F8** / #3888F8, light end **#70C8F8 – #7CC0F9**
- Royal-blue emphasis ("Multiple Languages", logo mark): **#0040C8 – #0050F8**
- Dark type: **#202038 – #282840** (dark indigo-grey); "Books" measured #383840
- Near-black button/logo text: **#080810**
- Progress-bar indigo **#4858F8** → #80A8F8, cyan accent **#00A9EB**

These are compressed-stream values, so expect ±2-4 per channel.

## Typography
A clean neo-grotesque at regular/medium weight with tight tracking. The logo shows a double-storey "a" and single-storey "g". Headline cap height is roughly 7-13% of frame height, with centred phrases of 1-4 words. The exact font is not identified. **Substitute: Inter 500 (SIL OFL)**, tracking -0.025 em.

## Motion language used as the target
- Blur-in entrances (≈14 px → 0) with a slight rise and an expo-like settle; blur + scale-up exits.
- Lines re-centre as words arrive.
- Large dark-bezel phones in steep perspective, cropped by the frame.
- Soft blue-grey shadows.
- Circle → rounded-square morph.
- Pointer-hand taps.
- Sparkle with a trail for the finale.
- A long, calm end-card hold.
